Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Viewport is a day planner app for Mac, iPhone, iPad and the web. It is a personal project made by Jake Lawrence ("I", "me"). This policy tells you which data Viewport uses, where Viewport keeps it, and how you can delete it.

In short: Viewport reads your Google Calendar from your own device to show it to you. Your calendar events are not sent to Viewport's servers. Viewport has no advertisements and no analytics. I do not sell your data.

1. Data that Viewport gets from Google

When you add a Google account with "Sign in with Google", Viewport asks Google for these permissions and gets this data:

Permission (Google scope) Data
openid, email, profile Your Google account ID, email address, name, and profile picture.
calendar.calendarlist.readonly The list of calendars in your account: their names, colors, time zones, and whether you can edit them.
calendar.events The events on those calendars: title, start and end time, location, description, organizer, guests and their answers, and color.

2. How Viewport uses Google data

  • To show your calendar. Viewport shows your events in the day timeline and the week view, next to your tasks.
  • To change events when you ask. When you create, edit or delete an event in Viewport, or answer an invitation (yes, no, maybe), Viewport sends that change to Google Calendar. Viewport does not change your calendar by itself.
  • To show which account is signed in. Viewport shows your name, email address and profile picture in its settings.
  • To identify you for sync. If you use Viewport's sync, Viewport uses your Google account ID to find your tasks in Viewport's cloud storage (see section 4).

Viewport does not use Google data for any other purpose.

3. Where Google data is stored

Google Calendar data stays on your own device:

  • Calendars and events. The Mac, iPhone and iPad apps keep a copy of your calendars and events in a database on your device. This lets the app show them quickly and without a connection. The web version keeps them in memory only.
  • Sign-in tokens. The token that keeps you signed in to Google is stored on your device (in the Keychain on Mac, iPhone and iPad). It is not sent to Viewport's servers.
  • Name and profile picture. These are stored in the app's settings on your device.

Viewport's servers do not receive or store your calendars or your events. The app talks to Google Calendar directly.

4. Sync: data stored in Viewport's cloud

Viewport can sync your tasks between your devices. When you are signed in with Google and sync is on, this data is stored in Viewport's cloud storage, which is hosted by Supabase:

  • Your Google account ID and your email address, to identify your account.
  • Your Viewport tasks: titles, notes, priorities, deadlines, repeat rules, and whether they are done.
  • Your Viewport settings that follow your account (for example, section colors).
  • The name and type of each device that syncs (for example, "MacBook Pro", "macOS").

To prove who you are, the app sends a short-lived Google access token with each sync request. The server checks this token with Google and then discards it. The server does not store the token and does not use it to read your calendar.

5. Sharing

I do not sell, rent or trade your data. Viewport does not share your data with third parties, with these limits:

  • Google. The app sends your calendar changes to Google, because you ask it to.
  • Supabase. Supabase hosts the sync data in section 4 as a service provider.
  • Law. I will disclose data if the law requires it.

Viewport has no advertisements, no analytics and no tracking. Your data is not used to build, train or improve artificial intelligence or machine learning models. No person reads your data, unless you ask me for help and give permission, or it is necessary for security or to obey the law.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Viewport's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Remove a Google account in Viewport. Open Settings, then Accounts, and remove the account. Viewport then revokes its access with Google, and deletes the saved calendars, events and sign-in token for that account from your device.
  • Revoke access at Google. You can also remove Viewport's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete the app. If you delete Viewport and its data from your device, the local copy of your calendar is deleted too.
  • Delete sync data. Sync data (section 4) is kept until you ask me to delete it. Send an email to jake.lawrence24@gmail.com from the address of your account. I will delete your account and all its sync data within 30 days.

8. Security

  • All connections to Google and to Viewport's cloud storage use HTTPS.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the Keychain on Mac, iPhone and iPad.
  • Sync data is in a private database. Only Viewport's sync service can read it, and only for the account that proves its identity with Google.

9. Children

Viewport is not made for children under 13. I do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. If the way Viewport uses Google data changes, Viewport will ask for your agreement before it uses your data in the new way.
