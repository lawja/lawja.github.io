What Viewport is

Viewport is a planner app for Mac, iPhone, iPad and the web. It is built around one idea: the day. You pick a day and see the events of that day on the left and the tasks of that day on the right. A score shows how much of the day's work is done.

  • Recurring tasks (daily, weekly, monthly, or a custom rule) with streaks.
  • Lists for today, tomorrow, and "eventually", with priorities and deadlines.
  • A day timeline and a week strip that show your calendar events.
  • Optional sync of your tasks between your devices.

Viewport is a personal project made by Jake Lawrence. It is free. It has no advertisements and no analytics.

How Viewport uses your Google account

Viewport shows calendars from Google accounts that you add with "Sign in with Google". Viewport asks for these permissions:

Permission Why Viewport needs it
Your name, email address and profile picture To show which account is signed in, and to identify your account when your tasks sync between your devices.
See the list of your Google calendars To list your calendars with their names and colors, so that you can choose which calendars Viewport shows.
View and edit events on all your calendars To show your events next to your tasks. To create, edit and delete events, and to answer invitations, only when you do that action in Viewport.

Viewport talks to Google Calendar directly from your device. Your calendar events are not sent to Viewport's servers. The Privacy Policy has the full details.
